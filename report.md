# Collocation Analysis of "junzi" in *The Analects* and *The I Ching*
In ancient China, the word “junzi” was used to judge a man who have the noble virtue and right behavior. From honorable emperors to ordinary people, no matter what belief of thought they followed, your character and behavior must be recognized by the society if you are called ”junzi”. But thousands of people have thousands of faces, there was no clear standard to definite what is “junzi”. Different people and schools held different ideas about what characters we have to be called junzi. In this article,I will search for the words that occurs with “junzi” based on the corpus and analyze the meaning of “junzi” in Analects and *The I Ching. At the same time, I compare the differences between the p-value and the LogDice, and carefully analyze the context by using KWIC keywords. Distinguish the difference between the true associations and the false associations caused by the set phase in ancient texts. Finally, analyze the differences in the meaning of "juzi" between the two texts.
The Confucianism had an indispensable ruling position in ancient China. Therefore, the ideas contained in Confucian classics are more representative and referential for research, because they are widely accepted by the majority of people and whole society.The Analects and the I Ching are both classic works included in the Four Books and Five Classics. They both belong to the core content, but they have significant differences in the time of their written and the core thought. Therefore, they are suitable for analysis and comparison.The Analects and I Ching used in this report were sourced from the Gutenberg Project. The research only extracted the main content of the original texts of the classics, removed the translator's prefaces, footnotes, annotations, appendices, etc. from the books, and only retained the original text of the classics in order to preserve the features of the texts before Qin dynasty.
After the processing of the text，This study did three analysis by using the window horizon = 5, window horizon = 10, and sentence search methods respectively. And used two indicators called the p-value and LogDice for test.The smaller the p-value is, the lower the probability of the two words occurring together, and the more statistically significant the co-occurrence phenomenon will be.The higher the LogDice value is, the more likely the two words occur together habitually and constitute an exclusive combination. Because of the evaluation logic difference between two indicators, the final ranking of the collocations will also be different.After generating the list of collocations, use the KWIC tool to retrieve the original sentences where collocations frequently occurred and carefully reading. This step is used to distinguish between two situations: one is the true connection between the words; the other is the fake associations in ancient texts. The data in the list cannot replace the author's thought in the text, so the relevant text must be placed back into the original context for verification.
As can be seen from the statistical results of zhouyi_sentences_window10.csv,The word "xiaoren" is the most closely associated with the term "juzi": the local co-occurrence observation value obs_local is 15, LogDice is 11.63, the corrected p-value is approximately 2.21×10⁻⁹, which is extremely low, indicating that this co-occurrence is not random. The KWIC index frequently appears sentences like "Gentleman follows the path of virtue, while the minister follows the path of corruption", which constitute a pair of opposing concepts. Other notable combinations include Daochang, Laoqian, Xi Ti, Ruo Li, Jin De Xiu Ye, Dao Xiao, Fu Zi Fei, Yi Shen, Yan Hua, Yu Xiang, Yi Xing, Yi Yin, Hu Ci, Bao Bian, Wu Lun, Zhi Guang. obs_local are all 2, and LogDice remains stable at around 9.02.These words collectively reflect: when a gentleman observes the changes in the universe, he acts based on the gua patterns and circumstances, cultivating virtue and reflecting on himself. Frequent combinations such as "advance virtue and pursue achievements", "toil and be humble", and "toil day and night" all point to conforming to the laws of nature and adapting to the changing times.In The Analects, we can clearly get many collocations involving “junzi” and morality, interpersonal relationships, and communication and conduct. The core characteristics of a "junzi" in Analects emphasis on virtue, the awareness of social responsibility, the establishment of one's self through benevolence and righteousness, and the insist on moral principles in daily communications.In Analects, "junzi" often appears with "xiaoren" .However, the contexts in which they co-occur are significantly different from those in I Ching: The contrast between "junzi" and "xiaoren" in Analects mainly involves value opposition at the level of personality and morality, distinguishing between social responsibility and profit;In the I Ching, the relationship between the “junzi” and the “xiaoren” is more of a contrast in the rise and fall of circumstances.Generally speaking, the difference between the concept of "junzi" in Analects and I Ching is that The Analects places greater emphasis on the moral cultivation and social responsibility of the "junzi", and pays more attention to the cultivation of moral character. I Ching, focuses more on people's understanding of the situation and pays more attention to doing the right things at the right time.The common point of both is that they both introduce "xiaoren" as negative examples to emphasize the rationality and correctness of their values.
This study also has several limitations. Firstly, the research only selected two extant texts, namely Analects and I Ching, resulting in a relatively small corpus. Moreover, the digitalized texts were not compared with various variant readings of ancient books or unearthed versions for verification. The sources are limited and need to be further enriched. Secondly, the setting of Horizon is a positional connection, not same to semantic correlation. No matter how Horizon is set, the algorithm focuses on character distance and does not understand syntactic boundaries and narrative logic. Statistical indicators such as LogDice can only measure co-occurrence correlation and cannot be directly equated with semantic collocation. Therefore, manual KWIC detailed reading is indispensable.Thirdly, in this study, we only used the keyword "junzi". There are numerous phrases for determining whether someone is a gentleman. By choosing only this one word, the scope of the study becomes relatively narrow.
